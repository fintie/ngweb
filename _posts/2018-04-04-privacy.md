<p class="paragraph-2">Your privacy is important to us. At Blockchain Group Wallet, we follow a few fundamental principles:</p>

<ul class="unordered-list-5">
 	<li>We don’t ask you for personally identifiable information (defined below). </ul>
<h3>HOW YOU ACCEPT THIS POLICY</h3>
<p class="paragraph-25">By using Blockchain Group Wallet, including downloading one of our mobile applications, visiting our website, you agree to the use, disclosure, and procedures outlined in this Privacy Policy.</p>

<h3>WHAT PERSONAL INFORMATION DO WE COLLECT FROM OUR USERS?</h3>
<p class="paragraph-3">The information we collect from you falls into two categories: (i) personally identifiable information (i.e., data that could potentially identify you as an individual) (“Personal Information”), and (ii) non-personally identifiable information (i.e., information that cannot be used to identify who you are) (“Non-Personal Information”). This Privacy Policy covers both categories and will tell you how we might collect and use each type.</p>
We do our best not to collect any Personal Information from Blockchain Group Wallet users. That being said, we may collect a some Personal Information from you when you communicate with us, including your contact information, such as your phone number, social media handle, or email address (depending on how you reach out).

Like other online services, we also collect a variety of Non-Personal Information, including:
<ul class="unordered-list-5">
 	<li>Information you create through the Blockchain Group Wallet’s website or mobile applications, including Wallet addresses.</li>
 	<li>Various analytics data, such as: (i) the IP address of the computer you use to access Blockchain Group Wallet; (ii) the type of browser software you are using; (iii) the operating system you are 2 using; (iv) the date and time you access or use Blockchain Group Wallet; (v) the website address, if any, that linked you to Blockchain Group Wallet; (vi) the website address, if any, you leave our website and travel to; and (vii) other non-personally identifiable traffic data.</li>
</ul>
<h3>WHEN DO WE COLLECT PERSONAL INFORMATION?</h3>
<p class="paragraph-4"><em>When You Contact Us.</em></p>
We may collect certain Personal Information if you choose to contact us or if you report a bug or other error with Blockchain Group Wallet. This may include contact information such as your name, email address, and phone number. We, or companies that provide services on our behalf, may also collect certain Non-Personal Information from you, such as your locally hosted wallet (a “Wallet”) addresses.

<em>Information We Automatically Collect</em>

Users who visit our website or use our application may have their device’s IP address logged for the purpose of generating anonymous statistics or troubleshooting the performance of our web servers. Your IP address will not be used to track or identify you, but may be used to determine your geographic location in order to determine which of our services you are presented with. Users of our website or mobile applications will receive an anonymous unique device id (“UDID”) for the purpose of identifying the device to Blockchain Group Wallet servers. This UDID will not be tied to users’ identities, but will be used for debugging purposes and to differentiate devices when users access our Services using multiple devices.

<em>Third Party Services</em>

Certain features on Blockchain Group Wallet rely on various third-party products and services (collectively “Third Party Services”), such as the Ethereum network, Google’s Android application platform, Apple’s application platform, Coinbase, Changelly, and Shapeshift. If you authorize us to connect with these Third Party Services, these services may collect certain Personal Information, such as your name, email address, phone number, and any other information that the Third Party Service makes available to us. Please note that your use of these Third Party Services is governed by their respective Terms of Service and Privacy Policies. We use and disclose any collected information in accordance with our own Privacy Policy.

<p class="paragraph-6">Our website does not use cookies, targeted advertising, or third-party behavioral tracking. We do not intentionally track users in any way. Your I.P. address will not be used to track or identify you, but may be used to determine your geographic location to determine which of our services you are presented with.
