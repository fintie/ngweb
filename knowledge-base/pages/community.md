*AUSTRALIA OPC NETWORK*

## One person can build a business. A network can help it grow.

OPC means One Person Company: a business led by one operator, supported by AI, automation and external specialists where needed.
We are exploring an Australian network for people building this way. The proposed starting point is simple: small-group conversations, shared implementation experience and opportunities to collaborate.
- Exchange tools, workflows and lessons from real projects.
- Find complementary expertise in development, industries and customer acquisition.
- Explore joint projects and introductions where there is a clear fit.
There is no membership fee or confirmed event schedule announced here. Expressing interest starts a conversation; it does not enrol you in a programme.
