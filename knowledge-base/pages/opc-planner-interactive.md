# OPC service-plan tool (interactive part of /opc)

Route: https://nextgenius.com.au/#/opc · Runs fully in the browser with templates (no AI API, nothing stored).

## Inputs
1. What can you help with? (text) · 2. Who would you like to help? (text) · 3. What specific problem will you solve? (text) · 4. Delivery format (select: Review & action plan / Setup & implementation / Content & research package / Training & coaching) · 5. Hours per week (select: 3–5 / 5–10 / 10–20 / 20+)

Example prefill ("Try an automation example"): {"expertise":"workflow design and AI automation","customer":"small Australian service businesses","problem":"too much time spent handling enquiries and follow-ups","format":"setup","hours":"5–10"}

## Output sections
Positioning · Your first package · Delivery workflow · Pricing structure · Where AI can help · Your first week · Service page copy · Boundaries

## Output actions
Copy plan · Download .txt · Discuss this plan with NextGenius (mailto draft)
