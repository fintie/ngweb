Built around your business

Services built around your business, not technical jargon.

We help small and medium businesses turn AI, automation and software into practical changes your team can use.

Work out where AI fits in your business, what to do first, and what results are realistic.

  • A review of where AI can help your operations, sales, service and admin teams
  • A practical plan with priorities, costs and next steps
  • Advice on when an existing product is enough and when to build your own

Put what your business knows where staff can find it, so they spend less time chasing answers and repeating work.

  • An assistant that finds answers in your own policies, procedures, templates and documents, and shows where they came from
  • A help desk for staff questions, new starters and quicker decisions
  • Answers for your customers too, where that makes sense

Connect your business systems and remove the manual handovers that slow teams down and cause avoidable errors.

  • Assistants that can work across several of your systems, not only one
  • We automate repetitive tasks, follow-ups, notifications and status updates
  • We connect your customer records, operations, finance and support tools so information moves between them

A personal or business assistant that helps you find information, handle routine tasks and follow things through.

  • Set up with OpenClaw and tailored to founders, managers or teams
  • Linked to your documents, systems and daily processes
  • Help with secure rollout, testing and getting your team using it

Build software that fits your business instead of forcing the business to fit a generic tool.

  • Internal tools for service delivery, approvals, tracking, and reporting
  • Web platforms, customer portals, dashboards and admin systems
  • Improvements to the products and systems you already have

We stay involved after launch, so the solution keeps improving instead of sitting unused.

  • Start with a small test version, then roll out in stages
  • Changes based on user feedback and business results
  • Ongoing advice as your needs change
