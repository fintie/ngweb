## Starting offers to make concrete

1. **Business workflow review.** For a business with a slow or repetitive process. Proposed outputs: a process map, current workload baseline, feasibility and risk observations, and a recommended next step. Separate the introductory conversation from a paid assessment. Agree scope and pricing before publication.
2. **One-workflow pilot.** For an agreed problem with suitable inputs. State what is being built, which systems are involved, who reviews the output, how the pilot will be judged and what happens afterwards. Website or software projects can also be scoped directly when the need is already clear.
3. **Practical team workshops.** Use the prototype's three tracks as proposed packages: AI foundations, automating a workflow and preparing a knowledge assistant. Show intended audience, preparation, practical exercises and take-away materials. Confirm facilitators, delivery format, duration, group size and price before presenting these as bookable products.
4. **Governance with Altamir.** Offer an appropriate governance conversation and partner pathway. Explain how governance advice fits the delivery project and who delivers it. Do not rename a partner's service as a NextGenius-created methodology.

Avoid inventing Australian prices by converting Swiss prices. Estimate local delivery cost, partner charges, support obligations and target margin, then agree the actual offer.

Retain the quiet layout. Information hierarchy and the next action should carry the page, with no new animations required.

## Conversion and follow-up

- Business enquiry: name, work email, business, broad problem and preferred next step. Confirm receipt and send it to a named owner.
- Workshop enquiry: team size, experience level, topic and preferred format. Confirm the request, not an unconfirmed booking.
- Community interest: email and interests, with a distinct choice for future updates. Show dates and venues only when confirmed.
- Preserve direct email access. Old planner addresses now redirect to Partners. Keep source/campaign information with the enquiry without putting names, messages or email addresses in analytics events.
- Add scheduling if a real calendar and availability owner exist. Otherwise use an honest request-a-call flow with a tested confirmation and follow-up process.

## Release sequence and measurement

**First release:** agree the offer scope and partner roles, rewrite the homepage, add partner information, expand the existing services hub, package workshops, and deliver working enquiry capture with separate community registration. Validate desktop/mobile layouts, internal links, submissions, confirmations and the responsible person's receipt of each type of enquiry.

**Next release:** add an agreed governance offer page and one or two problem-specific pages supported by actual sales conversations. Improve partner referrals and shared delivery opportunities using qualified follow-up. Add partner case material as it becomes available.

**After evidence:** consider an AI opportunity self-check, further industry pages or paid acquisition. Use separate conversion paths for established businesses, workshop organisers and community participants.

Track three separate outcomes:

| Goal | Primary measure | Follow-through |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Consulting and automation | Qualified business enquiries and completed discovery conversations | Proposals, won projects and gross contribution |
| Workshops | Qualified team enquiries and paid bookings | Attendance, participant feedback and later delivery enquiries |
| Community | Confirmed registrations and attendance | Repeat participation and voluntary movement into workshops or business enquiries |

Record source, chosen path and enquiry status. Review the first four weeks after launch; if volume is too low for stable rates, use actual conversations and usability findings rather than declaring a statistical winner. Compare equivalent periods and record other changes in traffic or outreach.

Judge return using incremental gross contribution less implementation and ongoing costs. Page views and CTA clicks are diagnostic measures, not revenue. Extend the work that produces qualified conversations and useful participation; revise or defer what does not.
