# Company estimates and implemented carousel order

Reviewed 2 November 2026. The user explicitly requested estimates for private businesses and ordering by those estimates. All values below are AUD. These are internal inputs for sequencing logos; the website does not display the figures.

The comparison uses approximate equity/business-value proxies. Public entries use market equity, private entries use stated scenarios, and the charity uses reported net assets. It is not a uniformly audited net-worth comparison. Parent/group scopes are explicit: a parent value is not a standalone brand appraisal. Ranges are judgement scenarios, not statistical confidence intervals. Overlapping ranges mean relative positions can change under reasonable assumptions.

## Public-company correction

ICBC's [2025 annual report](https://v.icbc.com.cn/userfiles/Resources/ICBCLTD/download/2026/2025AnnualReport.pdf), printed page 97 / PDF page 99, confirms **269,612,212,539 A shares** and **86,794,044,550 H shares**, unchanged during 2025. A single H-share-based quote applied to all shares is not the correct combined-class calculation.

The [A-share close](https://stockanalysis.com/quote/sha/601398/history/) was CNY8.75 and [H-share close](https://stockanalysis.com/quote/hkg/1398/history/) HKD7.90 on 30 October 2026. At indicative USD/CNY6.6574 and USD/HKD7.8449, combined ordinary equity is approximately **US$441.76bn / A$628.27bn**. Sources: [CNY FX](https://www.exchangerates.org.uk/USD-CNY-EXCHANGE-RATE-HISTORY.html), [HKD FX](https://www.exchangerates.org.uk/USD-HKD-exchange-rate-history.html). Rounded USD/AUD1.4222 is used throughout. Share counts assume no subsequent material ordinary-share change; markets close at different times.

Coca-Cola's [October 2026 10-Q](https://investors.coca-colacompany.com/filings-reports/all-sec-filings/content/0001628280-26-067844/ko-20261002.htm) reports 4,301,902,335 shares at 26 October. The [30 October close](https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/ko/history/) was US$94.08, giving approximately **US$404.72bn / A$575.60bn**. ICBC therefore leads on this combined-class market-equity calculation. This differs from accounting net assets.

## Private-business models

**Alipay:** [Ant Group](https://www.antgroup.com/en/business-development) is the operator. Alibaba's [2023 filing](https://www1.hkexnews.hk/listedco/listconews/sehk/2023/0810/2023081000990.pdf) records Ant's repurchase-implied RMB567.1bn equity value, approximately US$78.54bn then. The A$100–220bn range and A$150bn centre are a broad, low-confidence **Ant Group** scenario anchored to that historical deal. They are not a newly reported transaction or a standalone Alipay valuation.

**LOTTE × PHANTACi:** Hotel Lotte's [2024 consolidated statement](https://www.lottehotel.com/cms/asset/2025/03/31/25069/52%EA%B8%B0%20%EA%B2%B0%EC%82%B0%EA%B3%B5%EA%B3%A0%20%EC%9E%AC%EB%AC%B4%EC%83%81%ED%83%9C%ED%91%9C.pdf), PDF page 1, reports controlling equity **KRW10,393,144,365,218**, approximately A$11.08bn at assumed KRW938/AUD. A roughly 0.7–1.35× book-equity scenario yields A$8–15bn. [JCR's 2026 rationale](https://www.jcr.co.jp/download/f037d8c8c77f372f58fc0cf3020780ccc99482f446027849f8/25i0109_f.pdf) confirms its duty-free/hotel business and asset-revaluation context. This values neither PHANTACi nor the collaboration and does not substitute all LOTTE Group assets. Later transactions and market discounts remain uncertain.

**Zambrero:** [Preqin](https://www.preqin.com/data/profile/asset/zambrero-pty-ltd/532281) reports a historical A$700m valuation in 2023. The [transaction adviser](https://www.occstrategy.com/en/occ-advises-metric-capital-partners-on-acquisition-of-mexican-restaurant-chain/) described 245 restaurants; a [2026 CEO interview](https://www.restaurantbusiness.com.au/inside-zambreros-next-chapter-500-restaurants-global-expansion-and-a-new-leadership-bench/) reports 365. A constant-value-per-store scenario gives 700m × 365/245 ≈ A$1.04bn; a roughly 15% haircut gives the rounded A$900m centre. Store count is not profit, so A$650m–1.2bn remains a scenario. The investment amount is not the valuation.

**85°C:** the [franchise filing](https://www.nasaaefd.org/Franchise/Actions/DownloadFile.ashx?DocId=134722&isRegistered=true) identifies listed Gourmet Master as parent. [Recent market data](https://www.marketscreener.com/quote/stock/GOURMET-MASTER-CO-LTD-7862990/) puts the parent around US$462m, roughly A$657m, rounded to A$660m. It does not value the Australian franchise separately. Its range overlaps Zambrero's estimate.

**Mackas:** [AgriFutures evokeAG](https://www.evokeag.com/the-story-telling-power-of-agritech-how-data-is-helping-mackas-australian-black-angus-beef-tell-a-story-thats-worth-hearing/) reports eight farms over about 6,400ha and roughly 4,000 cattle. [MLA](https://www.mla.com.au/news-and-events/industry-news/climate-resilience-grounded-in-pasture-diversity/) confirms the farming operation. [Bendigo Bank](https://www.bendigobank.com.au/siteassets/business/industries/agribusiness/agriculture-insights/_reports/2026-farmland-values-report.pdf) gives a NSW 2025 farmland median of A$9,884/ha. Central scenario: (6,400 × 9,884 land + 4,000 × assumed A$1,800 cattle + assumed A$2m equipment) × (1 − assumed 30% debt/claims) = **A$50.72m**. Varying land at A$7k–13k/ha, livestock/plant and assumed debt at 20–60% yields about A$20–80m. Operated area may include leased/separately owned land; this is conditional backing of the aggregate family operation, not an appraisal of the trading brand or founder wealth. Generic directory figures were rejected.

**Starlight:** the [2025 financial report](https://downloads.ctfassets.net/5c0byp1iji8d/4Ws17wCkDm7KSLBI6K1Lzi/62a5a9207cddc78f352409f97d0fe6c3/Starlight_2025_Annual_Review_Report.pdf), printed page 29 / PDF page 31, reports assets A$21,959,796 less liabilities A$5,614,753 = **A$16,345,043 net assets**. Extracted and checked visually. This is a charity balance-sheet snapshot, not a commercial sale value, distributable wealth or a current 2026 appraisal.

**MGI Entertainment:** its [company profile](https://www.linkedin.com/company/mgi-entertainment) describes 30 specialists. Assume A$200k–350k net fee revenue per specialist, 15–25% normalised EBITDA, and 3–5× EBITDA. Centre: 30 × A$250k × 20% × 4 = **A$6m**. Revenue/margins are assumptions; talent/media pass-through billings are excluded. This is not Media and Games Invest, Miss Grand International or MGI Tech.

**Luma Research:** its [company profile](https://www.linkedin.com/company/luma-research) gives an 11–50 employee band and around 11 linked members; the [company site](https://www.lumaresearch.com/who-we-are) confirms advertising research. Assume 10–25 FTE, A$220k–320k net revenue/FTE, 15–25% margin and 3–5× EBITDA. Centre: 15 × A$250k × 20% × 4 = **A$3m**. These are scenario inputs, not reported financials. Linked members are not audited headcount. This is not Luma AI.

The agency multiples are conservative small-firm scenarios informed by [FE International's agency benchmarks](https://www.feinternational.com/blog/agency-valuation-multiples-in-2026-marketing-creative-and-dev-shop-benchmarks), not direct comparable sales of MGI or Luma. Unknown net debt is assumed neutral solely to make an ordering proxy; actual debt/cash could materially alter equity value. Both estimates have very low confidence and overlapping ranges.

**CHEFIN:** its [investor page](https://chefin.com.au/corporate/investors/) mentions A$6m marketplace activity without a clear period. It is not a valuation or net revenue. Assume annual GMV A$2m–6m, a 15–25% take rate and a chosen 2–4× net-revenue multiple. Centre: A$4m × 20% × 3 = **A$2.4m**; range A$0.6–6m. Annualisation, take rate, multiple and neutral net debt are unverified assumptions. No disclosed fundraising valuation was confirmed.

**Sushi Densha:** [Rouse Hill Town Centre](https://www.rhtc.com.au/stores-services/sushi-densha) confirms the venue. Assume A$2.5m turnover, 12% normalised owner earnings and 2.5× earnings = **A$750k**, range A$200k–1.5m for trade, lease and margin uncertainty. No unverified other venues or freehold added.

**Yasaka Ramen:** the [ABN record](https://abr.business.gov.au/ABN/View?id=86164677650) identifies the name under Glory Sydney Pty Ltd; it does not establish current trading or consolidated venue count. Conditional one-operating-venue scenario: A$1.8m turnover × 10% normalised owner earnings × 2.5 = **A$450k**. A$0–900k explicitly allows no continuing goodwill without asserting closure. No unverified venue group/freehold added. Both restaurants assume neutral net debt, have unverified financial inputs and very low confidence. The small-venue multiples are informed by [Strategic Transactions](https://strategictransactions.com.au/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/Food-and-Beverage-Market-Update-2023.pdf) and [Australian restaurant transaction guidance](https://www.businessforsale.com.au/business-advice/the-ultimate-guide-to-buying-a-restaurant-business).

## Reproducibility

`estimates.json` records all values, ranges, scopes, methods, assumptions and sources. `prepare-assets.cjs` sorts central AUD values and generates `site/src/data/clients.js`. The generated public data contains only company/logo metadata and order. The estimates stay in this research directory and do not become public copy.
